AI Governance Committee Meeting Minutes

Meeting Date:
January 20, 2026

Purpose:
Review production deployment of the Customer Support Chatbot.

Attendees:

* Chief Risk Officer
* Director of Information Security
* Customer Service Director
* Data Governance Manager

Agenda

1. Review AI Risk Assessment.
2. Review Security Assessment.
3. Review Privacy Review.
4. Discuss monitoring approach.
5. Vote on production approval.

Discussion Summary

Committee members agreed the chatbot provides operational value while maintaining acceptable residual risk through human escalation, monitoring, and restricted access to customer information.

Decision

Approved for Production

Conditions

* Weekly monitoring reports
* Quarterly governance review
* Annual AI Risk Assessment update
* Immediate reporting of significant AI incidents

Action Items

* Register production deployment.
* Schedule first quarterly review.
* Deliver monitoring dashboard to committee.
