AI Monitoring & Incident Response Procedure

## Purpose

Provide a consistent process for monitoring production AI systems and responding to incidents.

---

## Monitoring Activities

Weekly

* Review response quality
* Review customer complaints
* Verify system availability

Monthly

* Review model performance
* Review security logs
* Review operational metrics

Quarterly

* Governance Committee review
* Risk assessment validation

---

## Incident Categories

### Low

Minor quality issue with minimal customer impact.

### Medium

Repeated inaccurate responses affecting multiple customers.

### High

Security breach, privacy incident, or widespread incorrect recommendations.

---

## Response Process

1. Log the incident.
2. Notify the AI System Owner.
3. Assess severity.
4. Implement corrective actions.
5. Validate system performance.
6. Document root cause.
7. Present significant incidents to the AI Governance Committee.

---

## Escalation Criteria

Immediate escalation is required when:

* Customer data is exposed.
* The AI system produces harmful or misleading financial guidance.
* Security controls fail.
* Regulatory obligations may be affected.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Lessons learned from incidents will be incorporated into future risk assessments, governance reviews, and system improvements.
