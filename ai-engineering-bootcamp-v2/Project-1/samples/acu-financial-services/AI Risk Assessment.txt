AI Risk Assessment

## AI System

Customer Support Chatbot

---

## Business Purpose

Reduce customer wait times while improving consistency of responses.

---

## Identified Risks

### Risk 1

Hallucinated responses.

Potential Impact:

Customers receive inaccurate financial information.

Mitigation:

* Approved knowledge base
* Human escalation
* Response monitoring

Residual Risk:
Medium

---

### Risk 2

Disclosure of sensitive information.

Mitigation:

* Role-based access
* Data masking
* Logging

Residual Risk:
Low

---

### Risk 3

Prompt Injection

Mitigation:

* Prompt validation
* Input filtering
* Security monitoring

Residual Risk:
Medium

---

## Human Oversight

Customer service representatives review escalated interactions and investigate customer complaints.

---

## Review Schedule

Risk assessment reviewed annually or following significant system changes.
