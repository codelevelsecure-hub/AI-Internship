AI Governance Policy

## 1. Purpose

Acu Financial Services is committed to the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve customer service, operational efficiency, and business decision-making. This policy establishes governance requirements for all AI systems developed, purchased, or deployed by the organization.

The objectives of this policy are to:

* Promote responsible AI practices.
* Reduce operational and regulatory risk.
* Protect customer information.
* Ensure appropriate human oversight.
* Align organizational practices with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

* Employees
* Contractors
* Third-party AI services
* Machine learning models
* Generative AI applications
* Vendor-provided AI solutions

---

## 3. AI Governance Principles

Acu will ensure AI systems are:

* Lawful
* Fair
* Transparent
* Reliable
* Secure
* Privacy-preserving
* Subject to human oversight

---

## 4. Roles

### AI Governance Committee

Responsible for:

* Reviewing High-Risk AI systems
* Approving production deployment
* Reviewing AI incidents
* Monitoring organizational AI risk

### Business Owner

Responsible for:

* Business justification
* Operational oversight
* Accepting business risk

### Information Security

Responsible for:

* Security reviews
* Vulnerability assessments
* Security monitoring

### Data Governance

Responsible for:

* Data quality
* Data classification
* Privacy reviews

---

## 5. AI Risk Classification

### Low Risk

Examples:

* Document summarization
* Internal productivity assistants

### Medium Risk

Examples:

* Customer support chatbot
* Internal knowledge assistant

### High Risk

Examples:

* Credit risk scoring
* Fraud detection
* Customer eligibility decisions

---

## 6. Minimum Deployment Requirements

Every AI system must have:

* AI Risk Assessment
* Security Review
* Business Owner Approval
* AI Inventory Registration

High-Risk AI additionally requires AI Governance Committee approval.

---

## 7. Monitoring

Production AI systems shall be monitored for:

* Accuracy
* Reliability
* Customer complaints
* Security events
* Performance degradation
